═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                           KEY PRINCIPLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. TRANSLUCENCY: Everything is see-through to some degree
2. FROSTED BLUR: Backdrop-filter creates depth
3. MINIMAL CHROME: Only essential UI elements
4. CONTENT FIRST: Gallery is the hero, controls recede
5. SPATIAL RHYTHM: Consistent 24px spacing creates calm
6. APPLE TYPOGRAPHY: SF Pro style, precise weights
7. SOFT SHADOWS: Low-elevation shadows suggest depth
8. HAIRLINE SEPARATORS: Ultra-thin dividers (1px)
9. GRADIENT FADES: Barely perceptible vertical gradients
10. INNER HIGHLIGHTS: Mimics light reflection on glass
